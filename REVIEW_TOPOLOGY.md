# Review topology v1.1 — source-bound review without self-acceptance

## Purpose and scope

Separate authorship, technical review, organizational independence, and scientific acceptance. A mathematically relevant source includes its statement, proof, assumptions, domain, normalization, and imported dependencies. An engineering-only change to custody, lookup, CI, or navigation is not a mathematical promotion unless it also changes a scientific claim or its accepted scope.

Ordinary engineering review can therefore allow an engineering-only merge while every scientific status remains unchanged. Emergency integrity repairs are likewise permitted under the existing contract, with source identity and follow-up review recorded. Neither an engineering APPROVE, a merge, nor green CI accepts a theorem or by itself closes the broader main#90 enforcement obligation. Existing nonauthor engineering review requirements are not waived by this distinction.

## Two-key mathematical rule

For a mathematical object whose authoritative acceptance predicate requires independent review, positive reviewed/controlling status requires BOTH:

1. An author object bound to repository, immutable full commit OID, exact paths and content/prose digests, with statement, domain, assumptions and dependency identities.
2. A qualifying nonauthor review bound to that same object and covering every interface used in the proposed acceptance. Organizational independence must separately meet the authoritative predicate; it cannot be manufactured by naming another session.

These conditions are necessary, not sufficient: unresolved required premises, other scientific gates, and authority rules still apply. A review record is evidence consumed by the existing authority, not permission for a reviewer or integrator to assign status.

A formula-preserving wording change can alter regularity, quantifiers, domain or imported hypotheses. Such changes stale the old review and require review of the new immutable head and prose digest. An unrelated-file commit does not automatically invalidate an unchanged reviewed object: a reviewer must explicitly attest the unchanged object/dependency identities against the new head. Do not carry approval across heads silently.

## Lineage: distinct agent is not organizational independence

Record the known author and reviewer provider, model family, agent/session identifier, authorship involvement and source exposure. Unknown values remain UNKNOWN. The shared GitHub account d6g8k5htny-coder does not identify the underlying provider. The cursor[bot] account identifies an application, not its selected model. Different Cursor bcIds show different agent sessions, not independent providers. A new OpenAI conversation can provide useful additional critique but is not organizationally independent of another OpenAI author.

Same-provider or same-model-family review earns no organizational-independence credit by default. Unknown provider/model identity also earns no such credit. An administrative allowlist, different tool label, or self-reported token does not change historical authorship or prove independent provenance. A future exception needs an explicit authoritative criterion and substantiated provenance; this policy creates none.

Nonauthor technical reviews remain useful even without organizational-independence credit. Report technical correctness findings, review completeness, and organizational independence separately rather than discarding useful review or overstating what it proves.

## Evidence contract, not another schema

Review evidence must preserve: review ID; claim/object ID; immutable reviewed commit; exact paths and prose/content digests; declared review scope; author/reviewer provenance and exposure; interface dispositions and reasons; execution/source evidence identities; timestamp; and predecessor review links where relevant. ACCEPTED, AMEND_REQUIRED, BLOCKED and REFUTED have their stated scoped meaning, not automatic program-wide effect.

The main#95 scientific-state work, currently exposed by mainPR98, is the intended serialization/integration owner. This file states requirements only; it defines no parallel machine schema, status registry, accepted-record directory or new authoritative Boolean. That pilot is not presumed integrated or sufficient merely because it exists.

As of this amendment, no verified lineage validator has been demonstrated by this document. Provenance declarations are attested metadata until validated by an actual deployed mechanism and evidence. Do not label an independence field validator-derived or cryptographically verified without that mechanism. Missing required provenance fails closed for organizational-independence credit.

Semantic digest, evidence digest, mechanical verification level, scientific status and review evidence remain distinct. CAS/SMT/proof-assistant output verifies its encoded statement at its actual trust boundary; translation fidelity and imported hypotheses still require their own review. Test success and a hash match are not substitutes for those obligations.

## Fail-closed review and correction

- Self-review cannot supply the missing nonauthor key. An unavailable qualifying reviewer leaves the mathematical object AUTHOR_SIDE/HOLD.
- AMEND_REQUIRED, BLOCKED and REFUTED are not acceptance. Partial review cannot accept a broader domain or dependent conclusion.
- A reviewer who edits the reviewed proof becomes a contributor to that amendment. The successor requires another qualifying review; the old criticism remains valid provenance.
- Changed source or dependency meaning requires reverse-impact revalidation of affected consumers, not cosmetic fingerprint refresh.
- A source-bound STOP/REPAIR pauses NEW expansion and consumption of the AFFECTED faulty source. Its author first addresses the original-constraint counterexample, publishes a successor, and requests re-review. Unaffected research and independent reviews may continue. Repair work itself is not prohibited.
- If the finding is contested, return an exact rebuttal or counterexample against the reviewed source. Repeated higher-order enumeration, unchanged tests, or a new branch name cannot answer a leading-order defect.

## Reciprocal work and bounded idle reassignment

Every identified participating agent should have a concrete nonauthor review available, including work produced by the coordinator. Assign one primary task and a qualified fallback, with exact source, requested interfaces, output location, and a non-overlapping write scope. Read-only review of another repository may be returned in the agent's own writable repository; do not enlarge permissions merely to post a verdict.

An assignment or @mention is not acknowledgment or proof that a model started. Require an acknowledgment identifying target, scope and agent provenance before marking ACTIVE. Follow the existing Work Claims lease convention: a claimed task without a source-bound update for 120 minutes is eligible for expiry/reassignment after checking for a fresh claim, commit or result. A run should also reassign work explicitly declined or blocked. Do not expire a live acknowledged claim merely because a different task would be convenient.

For an unacknowledged or expired review, offer it to the next eligible nonauthor agent rather than repeatedly pinging a usage-limited service. Preserve prior dispatch and findings, identify the reassignment reason, and prevent simultaneous edits. Tool refusal is UNDELIVERED, not an assignment; do not retry a blocked action through another route. Missing qualified capacity stays explicit, never converted into acceptance.

Use the existing Downstream Closure Loop for continuing coordination. Do not create duplicate schedulers, unbounded agent fan-out, or status-only catalog churn. In each bounded run prefer one actual review, falsifier, correction or reproduction over repeated watch notes. Release the claim when the scoped result and evidence have been posted.
